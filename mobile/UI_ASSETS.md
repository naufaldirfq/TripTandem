Superdesign TripTandem Welcome draft (ff2e5a46-7c7d-4d7c-8c7d-4b172f61067c).
They are decorative stock photos, never representations of actual app members.

Apple's provider mark is from the Simple Icons Apple SVG. Google uses its
four-color G mark. Provider marks identify their respective authentication
services; the Apple button is compiled into shared UI but shown only on iOS.
