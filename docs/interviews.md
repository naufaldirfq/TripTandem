# Phase 0 interview guide and findings log

The product plan requires at least five short interviews before Phase 0 exits.
This document is a ready-to-run script and an honest record of findings. It
contains no fabricated participants or conclusions.

## Recruiting and consent

Recruit people who have planned a group trip in the last 18 months or have
looked for compatible travel companions. Ask for 15 minutes, record notes only
with permission, and remove names/contact details from the findings log. Do not
collect passports, exact travel dates, home addresses, or payment information.

Opening script:

> I’m testing an early concept for coordinating group trips. I’m evaluating the
> workflow, not you. May I take anonymous notes? You can skip any question or
> stop at any time. I will not store identifying details.

## Five-question script

1. Tell me about the last group trip you planned. What was hardest to keep
   aligned?
2. Where did the plan live, and what became stale, duplicated, or lost?
3. How would you feel about inviting people into one shared itinerary? What
   would make you trust or reject an invite?
4. Would you ever join an open trip with people you do not already know? What
   profile, safety, privacy, and approval controls would you need first?
5. Which organizer task, if any, would be worth paying to save time on? What
   would you expect to remain free?

Use neutral follow-ups such as “What happened next?”, “How often?”, and “Why?”
Do not pitch the solution before questions 1–2 are complete.
